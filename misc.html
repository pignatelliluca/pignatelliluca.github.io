Thoughts

Occasional short reflections. Not a blog — more like margin notes.

December 2025

Giving the PhD colloquium talk on Plateau's problem reminded me why I love mathematics outreach. The question "why do soap films minimise area?" is elementary to state, yet opens into one of the richest chapters of 20th-century analysis, and besides, I had so much fun blowing soap bubbles to the audience.

January 2026

I really am not a fan of mindlessly saying that in the real world processes evolve in such a way to always minimize some kind of energy. What about local minima for an easy example? What about non-equilibrium thermodynamics, a theory which could predict the thermodinamical emergence of life, as a process that actually maximises entropy production.

April 2026

I had to buy a new monitor, and in the process of trying to understand all the different specifications, i'm now trying to become knowledgeable in colour theory and digital colour spaces, like sRGB and DCI-P3. Maybe it turns out to be useful, but for now i'm just curious.
