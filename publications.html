  1. 2026 arXiv Under Review — IFB

    Low regularity potentials in heterogeneous Cahn-Hilliard functionals

    R. Cristoferi, J. Deutsch, L. Pignatelli

    arXiv:2603.25369

    In this paper, we study the prototypical model of liquid-liquid phase separation, the Cahn-Hilliard functional, in a highly irregular setting. Specifically, we analyze potentials with low regularity vanishing on space-dependent wells. Under remarkably weak hypotheses, we establish a robust compactness result. Strengthening the regularity of the wells and of the growth of the potential close to the wells only slightly, we completely characterize the asymptotic behavior of the associated family of functionals through a \(\Gamma\)-convergence analysis. As a notable technical result, we prove the existence of geodesics for a degenerate metric and establish a uniform bound on their Euclidean length.

  2. 2025 arXiv Under Review — ESAIM

    Phase separation in multiply periodic materials with fine microstructures

    R. Cristoferi, L. Pignatelli

    arXiv:2506.14462

    We study a Cahn-Hilliard model for phase separation in composite materials with multiple periodic microstructures. These are modeled by considering a highly oscillating potential. The focus of this paper is in the case where the scales of the microstructures are smaller than that of phase separation. We provide a compactness result and prove that the \(\Gamma\)-limit of the energy is a multiple of the perimeter. In particular, using the recently introduced unfolding operator for multiple scales, we show that the taking the limit of all of the scales together is equivalent to taking one limit at the time, starting from the smaller scale and keeping the larger fixed.

  3. 2026 In Progress

    Phase separation in multiply periodic materials with fine microstructures: a second regime

    L. Pignatelli

    Continuation of the first paper addressing the asymptotic regime when \( \eta \ll \varepsilon \ll \delta\). In this case, the \(\Gamma\)-limit is an anisotropic perimeter, with anisotropy given by an expanding cell formula, whose potential is given by the homogenization in the smallest scale.

  5. 2026 In Progress

    Convergence of critical regime to sub- and super-critical regimes

    R. Cristoferi, L. Pignatelli

    In the study of liquid-liquid phase separation in periodic domain, three regimes pop up. One of these, the critical regime, is when the separation scale and periodicity scale vanish at the same rate. The goal of this paper is to study the convergence of the functional obtained by the $\Gamma$-limit in the critical case when $\varepsilon = L \delta$, in the cases when $L \to 0$ and $L \to \infty$, and prove that with these limits we obtain the functionals for the supercritical and the subcritical case respectively.
